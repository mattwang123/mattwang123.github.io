Research

I am interested in Natural Language Processing and Multimodal Intelligence, with a focus on making models reliable. My research asks: when a model tells us something, can we trust it and verify it? A model should be , , and . I study these properties in attributed generation, over scientific tables, under temporal shift, and across modalities, via rigorous evaluation, post-training, and neuro-symbolic methods. Ultimately, I aim to help people use AI in research, decision-making, and creative work with understanding and trust.
